BRIEF

Four fixed 4K traffic cameras watch the roundabouts on E 96th St. Between them are 0.5–1 km stretches no camera sees, with side streets and parking-lot entrances. For every vehicle that leaves one camera toward the next, the monitor proves, without deciding who is who, how many did not come out the other side within the time a straight drive takes, and which vehicles they can be.

ABOUT THIS REPLAY

Video, tracks and identities are from RoundaboutHD, a public dataset recorded by four real non-overlapping 4K cameras on E 96th St, Indiana (40 min of annotated footage; this replay uses the 10 minutes all four cameras recorded together). Per-camera vehicle tracks are the dataset's own annotations; the monitor never reads the cross-camera identity labels. Appearance comes from two open models used off the shelf: a VeRi-776 vehicle re-ID network (fast-reid) and DINOv2, plus colour. Time windows and the appearance gate are calibrated on one half of the recording and scored on the other half, then swapped. The map is OpenStreetMap. UAS-1 is notional: no aerial footage exists for this recording, so its search plan is shown but never reports a contact.
