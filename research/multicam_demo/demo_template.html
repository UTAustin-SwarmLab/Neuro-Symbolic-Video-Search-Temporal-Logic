Four cameras watch the ring road around a forward operating base. Each camera tips off the next one with an arrival window. When a vehicle fails to arrive, the monitor proves it without deciding which look-alike vehicle it was. When a camera cannot see, it reports the gap instead of "all clear".

What this replay is

Every camera frame is rendered by a simulator and processed from pixels by a classical detector and tracker; the monitor never reads ground truth. Cameras differ in white balance, exposure and scale, so look-alike vehicles cannot be told apart across cameras. Arrival windows and the appearance gate are calibrated on separate normal-traffic episodes. The VLM answers shown are recorded runs of Qwen3-VL-4B on frames from the same episode. This is synthetic evidence, not field video.
