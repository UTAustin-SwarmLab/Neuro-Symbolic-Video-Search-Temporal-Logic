# Perimeter handoff demo: research record

Built 2026-10-18 for the multi-camera demo and proposal due 2026-10-19. Independent of the Codex thread in `../active_verification/`; only the local Qwen3-VL weights there are read.

## Pipeline

- `world.py` builds the ring road, four cameras, three blind zones (G1–G3), a side track in G2, and five scenario types: normal, stop in G2, divert onto the side track, swap (a different vehicle joins from the side track), and degraded (dust over CAM-N).
- `render.py` draws a rectified overhead view per camera with per-camera white balance, exposure drift, scale error and sensor noise.
- `perception.py` runs background subtraction, blobs and tracklets from pixels only.
- `monitor.py` holds the identity-free handoff certificate (Hall deficit over gated departure–arrival graphs), coverage-aware UNVERIFIED, actions, and two baselines: committed re-ID assignment and count-only.
- `evaluate.py` calibrates on normal-traffic seeds 1000–1023 and evaluates disjoint seeds.
- `prepare_vlm_inputs.py` and `vlm_eval.py` run the off-the-shelf VLM baseline (Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct-4bit, local MLX).
- `export_demo.py`, `demo_template.html` and `build_demo.py` produce the demo page.

## Run history

1. `heldout_v1` (seeds 20000+, 40 per type, 600 link verdicts) exposed three problems. They are reported here, not hidden.
   - Simulator: vehicles in one lane could pass through each other inside a camera view; merged detections produced false "unexplained arrival" alerts. Fixed by single-lane car following, applied camera by camera.
   - Monitor: detections made while a camera was degraded were used as evidence, so dust fragments became "unexplained arrivals". Fixed by the stated principle that evidence observed while its own camera is degraded is inadmissible.
   - Committed baseline: returned all clear whenever a link had no departures. Fixed to flag unmatched arrivals.
   - v1 numbers (pre-fix) for the record: monitor 556/600 correct, 1/200 false all-clear, 43/480 false alarms; committed 504/600, 41/200, 55/480; count-only 480/600, 120/200, 0/480. Candidate set contained the vanished vehicle in 80/80 stop/divert episodes; committed tracker named it in 72/80.
2. `vlm_qwen4b_v1` on 40 v1 episodes: 11/40 verdicts correct; 0/40 correct vehicle counts at either camera; 1/24 disappearances detected; 9/24 false all-clear; "cannot_confirm" on 28/40, which explains its 8/8 on degraded episodes; 28 s per query on ~48 cropped frames while the machine was also running the evaluation.
3. `heldout_v2` (fresh seeds 30000+, same calibration seeds, fixed simulator and monitor): monitor 596/600 correct, 2/200 false all-clear (both look-alike swaps, seeds 33005 and 33037), 2/480 false alarms (30009 and 33013, on the CAM-S→CAM-W link); committed tracker 516/600, 2/200, 82/480; count-only 481/600, 119/200, 0/480. Candidate set contained the vanished vehicle in 80/80; committed tracker named the right one in 75/80. Perception 4.4 ms per frame on CPU, measured while the machine was heavily loaded.
4. `vlm_qwen4b_v2` on 40 v2 episodes: 11/40 correct, 0/24 anomalies detected, 10/24 false all-clear, 0/40 correct counts, 27/40 "cannot_confirm", 28.7 s per query, 8,257 prompt tokens. `vlm_qwen4b_extra` adds demo episode 31008, where it answered "cannot_confirm".
5. Demo page `perimeter_handoff_monitor.html` built from `picks.json` (six v2 episodes), published at https://claude.ai/artifact/6eBDJkpu78yb61n3jbL4Zk.
6. Demo page redone as a tactical map client (`tactical_template.html`, now the default template in `build_demo.py`). It has a full-screen fictional imagery basemap, an MGRS-style grid, simplified MIL-STD-2525 friendly sensor and unknown-track symbols, blind-zone geofences, tip-off ETA bands, last-known-position markers, a UAS tasking line with range and bearing, a video dock, alert toasts, and a drawer with alerts, sensors and analysis. It is styled after map-centric clients such as WinTAK but uses no product name or branding. The earlier light layout is kept as `demo_template.html`.
7. UAS tasking now goes to where a vanished vehicle can be, not to the blind road it vanished from. After a certified miss the page keeps two hypotheses: stopped somewhere on the blind road, or left by the zone's exit. The exit hypothesis grows as reach(t) = max plausible speed × (t − earliest exit time), using the speed bounds from calibration and an assumed 36 km/h cap on the unpaved track (simulated track speeds are 22–32 km/h). The UAS (144 km/h) flies to where reach() will be when it arrives, then sweeps back along the exit and both legs of the blind road. Contact is simulated as passing within 80 m of the true vehicle, with ground truth standing in for the UAS camera. Results for the three alert missions: stop 31008, contact after about 3 min on the G2 road; swap 33000 and divert 32003, contact 12–23 s after reaching the intercept point, 1.15 km out on the side track.

8. Real footage, 2026-10-18 evening, after the user asked for real data instead of a synthetic demo. Code is in `real/`, outputs in `results/real/`, the page is `corridor_handoff_monitor.html` (template `tactical_real_template.html`, build `build_real_demo.py`), published at https://claude.ai/artifact/FXZG1sDUySTFfrYSj1dQih with the four videos as files under `video/`.
   - Data: RoundaboutHD (Hugging Face `yl4300/RoundaboutHD`, not gated, 8.08 GB zip, SHA-256 in `real_data/roundabouthd/RoundaboutHD.zip.sha256`). Four non-overlapping 4K cameras at roundabouts on E 96th St (Indianapolis/Carmel border), 600 s each, recorded together. Map from OpenStreetMap via Overpass (`real_data/osm/osm_96th.json`).
   - `rhd_tracks.py`: per-camera tracks from the dataset's SCT annotations; global IDs joined from the MCVT file by box IoU (832 of 833 camera-tracks joined) and used only for calibration and scoring. Roundabout legs come from image-space gates read off start/end-point plots, because the GPS projection is unreliable near image edges (camera 3 put east-west traffic on the north leg).
   - Appearance: `rhd_embed.py` (DINOv2 ViT-S/14) and `rhd_reid.py` (fast-reid SBS R50-IBN trained on VeRi-776, weights from the fast-reid v0.1.1 release; only the classifier head is missing from the checkpoint). Separate venv `.venv-real` (torch 2.14.1).
   - `rhd_monitor.py`: two-fold time split (calibrate on one 300 s half, score the other, then swap). Windows `[0.8 * p5, 1.3 * p95]` of labelled through-traffic drive times per link; gate keeps 99.5% of calibration true pairs minus a 0.5-logit margin (chosen before scoring, not tuned). Four gates run: time only, DINOv2, re-ID, re-ID plus colour (logistic pair score).
   - Results (re-ID plus colour): 577 scored departures, 92 truly went dark. Monitor 66 claimed, 63 right, 3 wrong; committed re-ID with the same gates 66 claimed, 62 right, 4 wrong; count-only per minute 39 claimed, 31 right, 8 wrong. Unexplained arrivals: 102 true, 73 claimed, 71 right. Gate ablation (certified right / wrong): time only 8/0, DINOv2 15/1, re-ID 48/2, re-ID plus colour 63/3. The committed tracker nearly ties on this question; the honest difference is the guarantee and the candidate set (35 of 61 alerts pin one vehicle).
   - The 3 unsound claims, checked by eye (`scratchpad` contact sheet): 2:226 to 3:298 (same grey F-150, front view vs side view, gate rejected), 3:378 to 4:295 (same BMW i3, side vs rear), 2:197 to 3:264 (the dataset's own single-camera track switches from a silver pickup to a dark one; 21.7 s for 860 m is impossible).
   - `rhd_vlm.py`: Qwen3-VL-4B on 26 one-minute windows (15 with a vehicle that went dark, 11 without), 42 frames each from the 640x360 replay videos. 10/26 correct, caught 2/15, 12/15 false all-clear, 3/11 false alarms, 1 invalid JSON, departing count right 2/26, 36 s and about 10,100 prompt tokens per answer. The monitor on the same windows: 21/26, caught 11/15, 1/11 false alarm.
   - Not yet real: detection and tracking (tracks are dataset annotations), camera outages (UNVERIFIED is only tested on synthetic), the UAS (no aerial footage; the page shows a search plan with assumed speeds and never a contact).

## Known limits

Synthetic runs: synthetic pixels with a classical detector; closed map of exits; a look-alike swap that passes the appearance gate is undetectable by any camera-only method; occlusion between vehicles is avoided by the single-lane traffic model rather than handled by perception.
