ABOUT THIS REPLAY

Synthetic replay. Every camera frame is rendered by a simulator and processed from pixels by a classical detector and tracker; the monitor never reads ground truth. Cameras differ in white balance, exposure and scale, so look-alike vehicles cannot be told apart across cameras. Terrain, base layout and grid coordinates are fictional. VLM answers are recorded runs of Qwen3-VL-4B on frames from the same episode.
